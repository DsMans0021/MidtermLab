Material Balance · الموازنة المادية

Material balance is the law of conservation of mass applied to a process. This page covers the general procedure, basis selection, balances without and with reaction, limiting reactant, conversion, excess air, and combustion.

الموازنة المادية هي قانون بقاء الكتلة مطبّق على عملية. الصفحة دي بتغطي الخطوات العامة، اختيار الأساس، الموازنة بدون ومع تفاعل، المتفاعل المحدد، نسبة التحويل، الهواء الزائد، والاحتراق.

1 · The method

The four-step procedure

Every material-balance problem follows the same skeleton. If you get stuck, you almost always skipped step 1 or step 2.

كل مسألة موازنة مادية بتمشي على نفس الهيكل. لو وقفت، غالبًا نسيت الخطوة 1 أو 2.

  1. Draw and label the flowchart — boxes, arrows, known streams, unknown symbols.
  2. Choose a basis — usually the given stream amount, or 100 kg / 100 kgmol / 1 hour if none is given.
  3. Write balance equations — overall mass balance and component balances.
  4. Solve and check — units, degrees of freedom, and mass closure.
→

Basis · الأساس

A basis is a reference amount you choose. It is not a guess — choose the stream with the most known information. For gas problems, a molar basis (kgmol or mol) is usually easier. For liquids/solids, a mass basis (kg) is usually easier.

الأساس هو كمية مرجعية تختارها. مش تخمين — اختار الstream اللي فيه أكبر معلومات معروفة. في الغازات الأساس المولي (kgmol أو mol) أسهل. وفي السوائل/الصلبة الأساس الكتلي (kg) أسهل.

2 · Without reaction

Distillation, mixing, drying, evaporation

No reaction means each component is conserved: what enters in a component must leave in that component (or accumulate). For steady state, accumulation is zero.

بدون تفاعل معناها كل مكوّن محفوظ: اللي يدخل من مكوّن لازم يخرج منه (أو يتراكم). وفي الحالة المستقرة التراكم = صفر.

Overall mass balance
F = D + W
Feed = distillate + bottoms (steady state)
Component balance
F xF = D xD + W xW
x = mass or mole fraction of the component
Example 1 · distillation

10,000 kg/h of a solution containing 20% methanol is fed to a distillation column. Distillate contains 98% methanol and the bottoms contains 1% methanol (all by weight). Find the distillate and bottoms flow rates, and the percentage loss of methanol.

Basis: 10,000 kg/h feed Overall: 10,000 = D + W Methanol: 10,000(0.20) = 0.98D + 0.01W 2000 = 0.98D + 0.01(10,000 − D) 2000 = 0.98D + 100 − 0.01D → 1900 = 0.97D D = 1958.8 kg/h · W = 8041.2 kg/h Methanol lost = 0.01 × 8041.2 = 80.41 kg/h % loss = 80.41/2000 × 100 = 4.02%
!

Mass% vs mole% · وزن% ولا مول%

Read the problem carefully. If compositions are mass%, your balance is a mass balance. If they are mole%, your balance is a molar balance. Never mix the two in one equation without converting.

اقرأ المسألة كويس. لو التراكيز وزن% يبقى الموازنة كتلية. ولو مول% يبقى الموازنة مولية. متخلطش الاتنين في معادلة واحدة من غير تحويل.

3 · With reaction

Stoichiometry, limiting reactant, conversion

With a chemical reaction, use a molar basis and a balanced equation. The limiting reactant is the one that would disappear first; it controls the maximum product. The excess reactant is supplied beyond the stoichiometric requirement.

مع تفاعل كيميائي، استخدم أساس مولي ومعادلة موزونة. المتفاعل المحدد هو اللي هيخلص الأول؛ وهو اللي بيحدد أقصى ناتج. المتفاعل الزائد بيتغذى أكتر من الاحتياج الستويكيومتري.

Fractional conversion
f = moles reacted / moles fed
Percentage conversion = f × 100%
Excess reactant
% excess = (fed − theoretical)/theoretical × 100
Theoretical = stoichiometric requirement
Stoichiometric ratio
nA/nB = νA/νB
ν = coefficient in the balanced equation
Yield
% yield = actual product / theoretical product × 100
Theoretical from limiting reactant
Example 2 · ammonia synthesis

For N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃: (a) find the H₂ flow if 50 kgmol/h N₂ is fed in stoichiometric proportion; (b) find NH₃ produced if conversion is 30%.

Basis: 50 kgmol/h N₂ (a) H₂ required = 3 × 50 = 150 kgmol/h (b) N₂ reacted = 0.30 × 50 = 15 kgmol/h NH₃ produced = 2 × 15 = 30 kgmol/h Mass of NH₃ = 30 × 17 = 510 kg/h
4 · Combustion

Air, excess oxygen, and Orsat analysis

Combustion problems use air as the oxygen source. Dry air is usually taken as 21 mol% O₂ and 79 mol% N₂. Theoretical oxygen is the amount needed for complete combustion. Excess air is the extra supplied above theoretical. Orsat analysis reports dry flue gas composition on a volume (mole) basis, usually CO₂, O₂, and N₂.

مسائل الاحتراق بتستخدم الهواء كمصدر أكسجين. الهواء الجاف غالبًا 21% مول O₂ و79% مول N₂. الأكسجين النظري هو المطلوب للاحتراق الكامل. الهواء الزائد هو الزيادة فوق النظري. تحليل Orsat بيعطي تركيب غاز المداخن الجاف بالحجم (مول)، غالبًا CO₂ وO₂ وN₂.

Theoretical O₂
From the balanced combustion equation
C → CO₂ needs 1 O₂ per C; H → H₂O needs ¼ O₂ per H
Theoretical air
Air = O₂ / 0.21
Molar basis, dry air
Excess air
% excess = (O₂ supplied − O₂ theoretical)/O₂ theoretical × 100
Same as air excess percentage
Orsat basis
Dry flue gas: CO₂ + O₂ + N₂ (+ CO)
Water is removed before analysis
Example 3 · propane combustion

C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O. If 1 kgmol of propane is burned with 30% excess air, find the moles of O₂ supplied and the dry flue gas composition (neglect water).

Theoretical O₂ = 5 kgmol per kgmol C₃H₈ Excess O₂ = 0.30 × 5 = 1.5 kgmol O₂ supplied = 6.5 kgmol N₂ supplied = 6.5 × (79/21) = 24.45 kgmol Products (dry): CO₂ = 3, O₂ left = 1.5, N₂ = 24.45 Total dry = 28.95 kgmol %CO₂ = 3/28.95 = 10.36%; %O₂ = 5.18%; %N₂ = 84.46%
→

Wet vs dry · رطب وجاف

Orsat analysis is on a dry basis, so water is not included. If the problem asks for wet flue gas, add the water vapour produced by combustion. If it asks for dry, remove H₂O completely.

تحليل Orsat على أساس جاف، يعني الماء مش داخل. لو المطلوب غاز رطب، ضيف بخار الماء الناتج من الاحتراق. ولو جاف، شيل H₂O خالص.

Part II · 40 marks

المسائل · Problems

Show the basis, equation, substitution, and units.

P1
Find the volume occupied by 4 mol of an ideal gas at 2 atm and 27 °C. (R = 0.08206 L·atm/mol·K)
T = 300 K. V = nRT/P = (4 × 0.08206 × 300)/2 = 49.24 L.
P2
A gas mixture contains 4 mol N₂, 1 mol O₂, and 5 mol CO₂ at 3 atm. Find the partial pressure of O₂.
nt = 10 mol; y(O₂) = 0.10; P(O₂) = 0.10 × 3 = 0.30 atm.
P3
Calculate the pressure of 0.5 mol of a gas in 1.0 L at 300 K using the ideal gas law. (R = 0.08206)
P = nRT/V = (0.5 × 0.08206 × 300)/1.0 = 12.31 atm.
P4
10,000 kg/h of 20% methanol feed is distilled into 98% methanol distillate and 1% methanol bottoms. Find D and W by mass.
Overall: 10,000 = D + W. Methanol: 2000 = 0.98D + 0.01W. Substitute W = 10,000 − D: 2000 = 0.98D + 100 − 0.01D → 1900 = 0.97D → D = 1958.8 kg/h, W = 8041.2 kg/h.
P5
For N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃, 40 kgmol/h N₂ is fed with 150 kgmol/h H₂. (a) Which is limiting? (b) Find NH₃ produced at 25% conversion of the limiting reactant.
(a) N₂ needs 120 kgmol H₂; 150 is available, so N₂ is limiting. (b) N₂ reacted = 0.25 × 40 = 10 kgmol; NH₃ = 2 × 10 = 20 kgmol/h.
P6
C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O. 2 kgmol propane is burned with 20% excess air. Find O₂ supplied, N₂ supplied, and the dry flue gas composition.
Theoretical O₂ = 10 kgmol; supplied O₂ = 12 kgmol; O₂ left = 2 kgmol. N₂ = 12 × 79/21 = 45.14 kgmol. Dry products: CO₂ = 6, O₂ = 2, N₂ = 45.14; total = 53.14 kgmol. %CO₂ = 11.29%, %O₂ = 3.76%, %N₂ = 84.95%.
Part III · 20 marks

أسئلة مقالية · Written

W1
State Dalton's law and define mole fraction. · اذكر قانون دالتون وعرف الكسر المولي.
Dalton: total pressure of a non-reacting gas mixture equals the sum of partial pressures. Mole fraction yi = ni/nt, and Pi = yiPt.
W2
Explain why real gases deviate from ideal behaviour. · ليه الغازات الحقيقية تنحرف؟
Molecules have finite volume and intermolecular attraction. These matter at high pressure and low temperature, so PV ≠ nRT and Z ≠ 1. Van der Waals corrects both effects.
W3
List the four steps of a material balance. · اذكر خطوات الموازنة المادية.
Draw and label the flowchart; choose a basis; write overall and component balances; solve and check units/mass closure.
W4
Distinguish between theoretical air, excess air, and Orsat analysis. · فرّق بين الهواء النظري والزائد وتحليل Orsat.
Theoretical air is the stoichiometric amount for complete combustion. Excess air is the extra supplied above theoretical. Orsat analysis is the dry flue gas composition by volume (CO₂, O₂, N₂, CO).

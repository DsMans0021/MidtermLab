1 · Complex numbers

الأعداد المركبة

Rectangular form
z = a + b i
i² = −1
Conjugate
z̄ = a − b i
Modulus
|z| = √(a²+b²)
Argument
θ = tan⁻¹(b/a)
Check quadrant
Product z z̄
z z̄ = a²+b² = |z|²
Division
(a+bi)/(c+di) = (a+bi)(c−di)/(c²+d²)
Polar form
z = r(cos θ + i sin θ) = r eiθ
Multiplication
z₁z₂ = r₁r₂[cos(θ₁+θ₂)+i sin(θ₁+θ₂)]
Division
z₁/z₂ = (r₁/r₂)[cos(θ₁−θ₂)+i sin(θ₁−θ₂)]
De Moivre
zⁿ = rⁿ[cos(nθ)+i sin(nθ)]
nth roots
z1/n = r1/n[cos((θ+2kπ)/n)+i sin(…)]
k = 0, …, n−1
Powers of i
i, −1, −i, 1 (cycle of 4)

Before you submit · قبل التسليم

1) Complex argument quadrant correct. 2) De Moivre angle in the right units. 3) All n roots listed. 4) Remainder theorem sign correct. 5) Vieta signs correct. 6) Numerical method shows iterations. 7) Newton derivative correct. 8) Gauss row operations legal. 9) Back substitution checked.

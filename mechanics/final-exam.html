Part II · 42 marks

المسائل · Problems

7 marks each. Show the method.

P1
A = 3i + 4j, B = 6i − 8j. Find A·B, the angle between them, and the projection of A on B.
A·B = 18 − 32 = −14. A = 5, B = 10, cos θ = −14/50 = −0.28 → θ ≈ 106.3°. Projection of A on B = (A·B)/B = −1.4.
P2
F₁ = 80 N at 0°, F₂ = 60 N at 90°, F₃ = 40 N at 180°. Find the resultant.
Rx = 80 − 40 = 40 N; Ry = 60 N. R = √(1600+3600) = √5200 ≈ 72.1 N. θ = tan⁻¹(60/40) ≈ 56.3° above the positive x-axis.
P3
A force F = 100 N acts at point (3, 4, 0) m and is directed along the positive x-axis. Find its moment about the origin.
r = 3i + 4j, F = 100i. MO = r × F = (3i+4j)×100i = −400k N·m. The negative sign means clockwise about the z-axis.
P4
A 8 m simply supported beam has a pin at A and a roller at B. A 20 kN load acts at 3 m from A. Find the reactions.
ΣMA = 0: RB(8) − 20(3) = 0 → RB = 7.5 kN. ΣFy = 0: RA = 12.5 kN. Ax = 0. Check: 12.5 + 7.5 = 20 kN.
P5
A system has R = 10k N and MO = 20k N·m. Find the pitch and state the type of resultant.
p = (R·MO)/R² = (10×20)/100 = 2 m. R and M are parallel, so the system is a wrench: force 10k N plus couple 20k N·m.
P6
A 300 N block rests on a horizontal floor with μs = 0.25. A horizontal force P is applied. Find the minimum P to start motion.
N = 300 N (no vertical component of P). At impending slip, P = μsN = 0.25 × 300 = 75 N.
Part III · 18 marks

أسئلة مقالية · Written

W1
Explain the difference between the dot product and the cross product, with one statics application of each.
Dot: scalar, AB cos θ, used for angle and projection. Cross: vector, AB sin θ, direction by right-hand rule, used for moment r × F and for finding a perpendicular vector.
W2
Why is the FBD the most important step in an equilibrium problem? List four items that must appear.
The FBD defines the system and all external effects; missing a force or reaction makes every equation wrong. Items: body outline, weight, applied loads, support reactions, labelled unknowns, chosen axes.
W3
Explain the wrench and define pitch and central axis.
A wrench is a force plus a parallel couple. Pitch p = (R·MO)/R² is the advance per turn. The central axis is the line of action of R, through r₀ = (R×MO)/R², where the moment is minimum and parallel to R.
W4
State the laws of dry friction and explain why N is not always equal to the weight.
Fs ≤ μsN; Fk = μkN; friction opposes motion; μ depends on the surfaces. N comes from equilibrium perpendicular to the surface, so an inclined applied force or an incline changes N from mg.

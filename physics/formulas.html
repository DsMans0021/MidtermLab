4 · Temperature & thermometry

درجة الحرارة والترمومترات

Celsius ↔ Kelvin
T(K) = T(°C) + 273
Use 273.15 for precise work
Fahrenheit ↔ Celsius
TF = (9/5)TC + 32
Celsius ↔ Fahrenheit (reverse)
TC = (5/9)(TF − 32)
Kelvin ↔ Fahrenheit
TF = (9/5)(TK − 273) + 32
Linear thermometric property
T = T₀ + [(X − X₀)/(X₁₀₀ − X₀)] × 100 °C
X = resistance, length, pressure, emf…
Thermal equilibrium
Zeroth law: A~B and B~C → A~C
Defines temperature as a shared property
5 · Thermal expansion

التمدد الحراري

Linear expansion
ΔL = α L₀ ΔT
α in °C⁻¹ or K⁻¹
Area expansion
ΔA = 2α A₀ ΔT = β A₀ ΔT
β ≈ 2α
Volume expansion
ΔV = 3α V₀ ΔT = γ V₀ ΔT
γ ≈ 3α for isotropic solids
Liquid in a container
ΔVapparent = (γliquid − γcontainer)V₀ΔT
Apparent expansion is less than real expansion
Density change
ρ = ρ₀ / (1 + γΔT)
Density decreases as volume expands
Water anomaly
Max density at 4 °C
Water contracts from 0 to 4 °C, then expands

Before you submit · قبل التسليم

Check: (1) all temperatures in gas laws are in kelvin; (2) pressure is absolute, not gauge; (3) units are SI and consistent; (4) signs in ΣQ = 0; (5) phase changes use Q = mL, not Q = mcΔT; (6) M in vrms is kg/mol.

اتأكد: (1) كل الحرارات في قوانين الغاز بكلفن؛ (2) الضغط مطلق مش مقاس؛ (3) الوحدات SI ومتناسقة؛ (4) الإشارات في ΣQ = 0؛ (5) تغير الحالة بـ Q = mL مش Q = mcΔT؛ (6) M في v_rms بالكجم/مول.

Part I · 40 marks

الاختيار من متعدد · Multiple choice

اختر إجابة واحدة. 2 marks لكل سؤال.

1
The SI unit of stress is: · وحدة الإجهاد في SI:
b) N/m² (Pa). Stress = force / area.
2
A wire of length L and area A is stretched by ΔL. Young's modulus is:
b) FL/(AΔL). Y = (F/A)/(ΔL/L).
3
The pressure at a depth h in a liquid of density ρ is: · الضغط على عمق h:
b) P₀ + ρgh. Absolute pressure includes atmospheric pressure P₀.
4
A body floats when: · الجسم يطفو عندما:
b) FB = W. This implies ρfluidVsub = ρobjectVtotal.
5
In a hydraulic press, a force of 100 N on area 0.01 m² supports a load on area 0.5 m². The load is:
c) 5000 N. F₂ = F₁A₂/A₁ = 100 × 0.5/0.01 = 5000 N.
6
The zeroth law of thermodynamics implies that: · القانون الصفري يعني:
b) It defines temperature and justifies using a thermometer.
7
25 °C equals: · 25 °C تساوي:
c) 298 K. T = 25 + 273 = 298 K.
8
The coefficient of volume expansion γ for an isotropic solid equals approximately:
b) 3α. Area expansion is about 2α; volume expansion is about 3α.
9
Water has maximum density at: · الماء أعلى كثافة عند:
b) 4 °C. Between 0 and 4 °C water contracts; above 4 °C it expands.
10
Heat is: · الحرارة:
a) Heat is energy in transit; internal energy is stored energy.
11
During boiling, the temperature of a pure liquid at constant pressure:
c) Energy breaks intermolecular bonds instead of raising kinetic energy.
12
The energy needed to melt 0.5 kg of ice at 0 °C is: (Lf = 3.33×10⁵ J/kg)
b) 1.67×10⁵ J. Q = mLf = 0.5 × 3.33×10⁵.
13
Which is a good conductor of heat? · أي مادة موصل جيد للحرارة؟
c) copper. Metals have free electrons that carry energy efficiently.
14
Heat transfer through electromagnetic waves is: · انتقال الحرارة بالموجات الكهرومغناطيسية:
c) radiation. It needs no medium and travels through vacuum.
15
For an ideal gas at constant temperature, doubling the volume makes the pressure:
b) half. Boyle's law: P ∝ 1/V.
16
The ideal gas law is: · قانون الغاز المثالي:
b) PV = nRT. T must be in kelvin.
17
Temperature is a direct measure of: · درجة الحرارة مقياس مباشر لـ:
b) ½mv² = (3/2)kBT.
18
The internal energy of 2 mol of a monatomic ideal gas at 300 K is about:
b) 7.48×10³ J. U = 1.5 × 2 × 8.31 × 300 ≈ 7479 J.
19
At the same temperature, which gas has the highest rms speed? · أعلى سرعة rms عند نفس الحرارة:
c) He. vrms ∝ 1/√M, so the lightest gas is fastest.
20
If the absolute temperature of a gas is quadrupled, the rms speed:
c) doubles. vrms ∝ √T, so 4T gives 2v.
21
The first law of thermodynamics is: · القانون الأول للديناميكا الحرارية:
b) With W defined as work done by the system, ΔU = Q − W, or Q = ΔU + W.
22
A gas absorbs 500 J of heat and does 200 J of work. Its change in internal energy is: · غاز امتص 500 J وعمل 200 J، تغير طاقته الداخلية:
b) ΔU = Q − W = 500 − 200 = 300 J.
23
In an isochoric process, which statement is correct? · في عملية ثابتة الحجم:
b) Volume is constant, so W = 0; the first law gives ΔU = Q.
24
For an adiabatic process of an ideal gas: · في عملية أدياباتية لغاز مثالي:
c) No heat exchange, so Q = 0 and ΔU = −W. Compression (W negative) raises internal energy.
Part II · 40 marks

المسائل · Problems

5 marks لكل مسألة. اكتب الخطوات والوحدات.

P1
A steel wire of length 2.0 m and cross-sectional area 1.0 mm² stretches by 0.50 mm under a load of 100 N. Find (a) the stress, (b) the strain, (c) Young's modulus.
A = 1.0×10⁻⁶ m², ΔL = 5.0×10⁻⁴ m.
(a) σ = F/A = 100/1.0×10⁻⁶ = 1.0×10⁸ Pa.
(b) ε = ΔL/L = 5.0×10⁻⁴/2.0 = 2.5×10⁻⁴.
(c) Y = σ/ε = 1.0×10⁸/2.5×10⁻⁴ = 4.0×10¹¹ Pa.
P2
A hydraulic lift has input piston area 5 cm² and output piston area 400 cm². A force of 200 N is applied. (a) Find the output force. (b) Find the pressure in the fluid.
(a) F₂ = F₁A₂/A₁ = 200 × 400/5 = 1.6×10⁴ N.
(b) P = F₁/A₁ = 200/(5×10⁻⁴) = 4.0×10⁵ Pa.
P3
A block of density 800 kg/m³ and volume 0.02 m³ is fully submerged in water. Find (a) the buoyant force, (b) the weight, (c) whether it floats when released, and the fraction submerged.
(a) FB = ρwgV = 1000 × 9.8 × 0.02 = 196 N.
(b) W = ρgV = 800 × 9.8 × 0.02 = 156.8 N.
(c) FB > W, so it floats. Fraction submerged = 800/1000 = 0.80 (80%).
P4
A concrete bridge is 50 m long at 10 °C. Find its length change at 40 °C (α = 1.2×10⁻⁵ °C⁻¹). If the ends are fixed, find the thermal stress (Y = 2.0×10¹⁰ Pa).
ΔL = αL₀ΔT = 1.2×10⁻⁵ × 50 × 30 = 1.8×10⁻² m = 1.8 cm.
σ = YαΔT = 2.0×10¹⁰ × 1.2×10⁻⁵ × 30 = 7.2×10⁶ Pa.
P5
A 0.30 kg aluminum block at 150 °C is dropped into 0.80 kg of water at 20 °C. Find the final temperature. (cAl = 900, cw = 4190 J/kg·°C)
ΣQ = 0: 0.30×900(T−150) + 0.80×4190(T−20) = 0
270(T−150) + 3352(T−20) = 0
270T − 40 500 + 3352T − 67 040 = 0
3622T = 107 540 → T ≈ 29.7 °C.
P6
How much energy is required to convert 2 kg of ice at −10 °C into steam at 100 °C? (cice = 2090, cw = 4190, Lf = 3.33×10⁵, Lv = 2.26×10⁶)
Q₁ ice warms: 2×2090×10 = 4.18×10⁴ J
Q₂ melt: 2×3.33×10⁵ = 6.66×10⁵ J
Q₃ water warms: 2×4190×100 = 8.38×10⁵ J
Q₄ boil: 2×2.26×10⁶ = 4.52×10⁶ J
Total ≈ 6.06×10⁶ J.
P7
A gas occupies 2.0 L at 27 °C and 1.5 atm. It is compressed to 0.80 L at 3.0 atm. Find the final temperature in °C.
T₁ = 300 K. T₂ = T₁(P₂V₂)/(P₁V₁) = 300 × (3.0×0.80)/(1.5×2.0)
= 300 × 2.4/3.0 = 240 K = −33 °C.
P8
Find the rms speed of oxygen molecules at 27 °C (M = 32 g/mol, R = 8.31). Also find the average KE per molecule (kB = 1.38×10⁻²³ J/K).
T = 300 K, M = 0.032 kg/mol.
vrms = √(3×8.31×300/0.032) = √(2.336×10⁵) ≈ 483 m/s.
KE = (3/2)kBT = 1.5×1.38×10⁻²³×300 ≈ 6.21×10⁻²¹ J.
Part III · 20 marks

أسئلة مقالية · Written questions

W1
Distinguish between heat, internal energy, and temperature. · فرّق بين الحرارة والطاقة الداخلية ودرجة الحرارة.
Heat is energy transferred due to a temperature difference (transit, measured in J). Internal energy is the total energy stored in the random motion and bonding of particles (state function, J). Temperature is a measure of the average translational kinetic energy per molecule (K). Heat and internal energy are not the same; temperature is intensive and does not depend on mass.
W2
Explain why the temperature stays constant during a phase change. · ليه الحرارة ثابتة أثناء تغير الحالة؟
The added energy increases the potential energy of the molecules (separating them against attractive forces) rather than their average kinetic energy. Since temperature measures average kinetic energy, it remains constant until the phase change is complete.
W3
Compare conduction, convection, and radiation. · قارن بين التوصيل والحمل والإشعاع.
Conduction: energy transfer by molecular collisions/vibrations without bulk motion; needs a medium; dominant in solids, especially metals. Convection: energy transfer by bulk motion of a fluid; needs a fluid; natural (density differences) or forced. Radiation: energy transfer by electromagnetic waves; needs no medium; all bodies emit; rate ∝ T⁴.
W4
Use kinetic theory to explain why gas pressure increases when temperature rises at constant volume. · اشرح زيادة الضغط مع الحرارة بحجم ثابت.
Higher temperature means higher average molecular kinetic energy, so molecules move faster. They hit the walls more frequently and with greater momentum change per collision, producing a larger average force per unit area. At constant volume, the number density N/V is unchanged, so pressure rises in proportion to T (P/T = constant).
